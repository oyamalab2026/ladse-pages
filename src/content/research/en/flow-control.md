## Understanding and influencing a flow

Controlling the flow around a wing or vehicle could help reduce drag or provide a means of flight control. We investigate how devices such as plasma actuators and synthetic jets affect the surrounding flow.

Our work combines computational fluid dynamics and wind-tunnel experiments to understand the mechanisms through which these devices act.

## Moving between simulation and experiment

- Analysis and measurement of separated flows at low Reynolds numbers
- Flow control using plasma actuators and synthetic jets
- Pressure measurements and particle image velocimetry (PIV)
- Feedback control, including approaches based on deep reinforcement learning

By connecting actuator behaviour, changes in the flow and information obtained from sensors, we investigate how effective control can be achieved.
